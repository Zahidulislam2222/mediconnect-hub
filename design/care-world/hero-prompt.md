# Hero treatment — v1

Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: full-bleed website hero and potential image-to-video starting frame.
Reference: original project Blender render, care-world-v1.png. This is a composition and subject
reference, not a real clinic or an exact-geometry medical-device requirement.

Create a cinematic architectural photograph of an imagined connected-care pavilion, informed by
the three linked spaces in the reference: arrival desk, a quiet consultation space, and a
follow-up reading space. Translate the miniature into a beautifully detailed architectural
model: warm porcelain and pale travertine, finely fluted walls, brushed metal, natural linen,
subtle blue practical lights, small realistic indoor plants. Retain a coherent three-space
arrangement and open facades. Slightly lower three-quarter camera, strong depth and controlled
natural shadows, warm internal lighting against a deep blue-black studio environment. Confident
editorial architecture imagery, not a futuristic spaceship or glowing hologram. Keep the actual
pavilion in the right two-thirds; the left third should fall into quiet dark negative space for
large white HTML headline text. Landscape 16:9. Delicate atmospheric depth, sharply resolved
materials, restrained highlights. No text, letters, numbers, branding, watermark, interface
overlays, people, medical claims, floating particles or neon spectacle.

Production route: built-in image-generation tool; original Blender render as visual reference.
Any generated geometry is illustrative, not an exact reproduction of the GLB.
